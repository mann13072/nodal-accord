The resolution workflow

One continuous record, from the first signal to an export-ready agreement.

Every step builds on the one before it, and every decision is tied to the exact numbers it was based on.

  1. STEP 1

    Detect

    Disruptions are matched to the parts, plants and shared machines they affect. A twelve-week supply ledger shows when the shortage starts and how deep it runs.

    Exception queue ranked by units at risk
  2. STEP 2

    Evaluate

    Overtime, alternate sourcing, prebuild and hybrid plans are solved side by side. Each result states whether it is proven optimal or the best plan found within the time limit.

    Optimizes lateness first, then cost
  3. STEP 3

    Negotiate

    The chosen plan becomes a supplier offer with exact quantities, delivery weeks, premiums and payment terms. A counteroffer updates the inputs and triggers a fresh evaluation before any revision.

    Each offer version is locked to its terms
  4. STEP 4

    Approve

    Procurement signs off. Finance is added automatically above your approval threshold or when payment terms change. Approved agreements export as structured records for your ERP.

    Role-based approvals with a full audit trail
Capabilities

Built for decisions that hold up to scrutiny.

Optimization you can audit

Every plan reports its solver status, optimality gap and binding constraints, and shows where each price and premium came from. Anything the model had to assume is labelled as an assumption.

Confidential by design

Suppliers see only the offers addressed to them. Supplier machine hours and internal costs never reach the buyer, and the buyer's prices and approval thresholds never reach the supplier.

Shared capacity, handled correctly

When several customers depend on the same supplier machine, the optimizer respects its shared hour budget. Other customers' lanes are taken into account without revealing who they are or what they pay.

An assistant that shows its sources

Ask why a case is short or which plan is best. Answers cite the case and run they used, and any number that cannot be traced to the underlying data is flagged. The assistant reads and drafts; people decide.

Governance

Controls built into the workflow, not added afterwards.

Approvals, versions and permissions are enforced by the system on every action, including requests made through the AI assistant.

  • Tenant isolation on every record, every screen and every assistant tool call.
  • Immutable offer versions. Acceptance and approval must reference the exact terms that were reviewed.
  • Stale-input protection. If capacity or demand changes after acceptance, approval is blocked until the plan is re-evaluated.
  • Append-only audit trail of every decision, including attempts that were denied.
  • Controlled ERP hand-off. Agreements export as structured records; nothing is written to your systems without an explicit integration.
